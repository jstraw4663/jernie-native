Use inside a day group on Home, and in Agenda's type groups.

```jsx
<ItineraryRow time="12:10" duration="1h 30m" title="Jordan Pond House"
  sub="Popovers on the lawn · 0.4 mi away" photo={<img src={p} />} now
  badge={<Badge label="Next" tone="accent" />} />
```

Places get a photo; transport and unbooked slots get an icon tile.
