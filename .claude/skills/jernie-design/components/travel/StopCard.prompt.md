Use only in the home stop rail. The rail lifts off the hero photo by 56px and pins as the header on scroll.

```jsx
<StopCard kicker="Stop 2 of 3" name="Bar Harbor" dates="May 24 – 27 · 3 nights"
  status="Checked in" count="11 plans" active />
```

Inactive cards drop to 62% opacity and lose their ring. Dots below the rail are the only swipe hint — no arrows.
